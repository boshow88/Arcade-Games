How to play

  • Dots stream in from every edge. Weave through them — your score is your survival time.
  • Hold anywhere and drag: the dot moves with the pointer's motion, not to it. Lift and press again to re-anchor, so you can steer from a comfy spot.
  • Read the bullets: red ones are faster, and ones marked with an arrow track you (then straighten and fly off). A bullet's hitbox is exactly its circle.
  • You have a few lives — a hit costs one and briefly makes you invincible; at zero the run ends. P pause · R restart · M mute.
