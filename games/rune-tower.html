How to play

  • Press and drag an orb — it swaps along the path. Line up 3+ of a colour in a row or column to clear them and hit the foe.
  • Chain combos for more damage, and match the foe's weak element to hit harder. Heart orbs heal you.
  • Each spin is one turn; the foe strikes on its countdown. P pause · R restart · M mute.
